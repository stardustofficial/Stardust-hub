Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Stardust Hub. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use our application and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Stardust Hub, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Data: Email address, User Identifier (UID), cryptographic password hash, and profile details (Display Name, Profile Picture, Bio, Profession).
  • User Content: Text posts, comments, photos, videos, reels, and chat messages sent across our channels.
  • Technical & Device Data: Network handshake data, device type, connection parameters, and interactive log metrics required to operate real-time communications (PeerJS/WebRTC).

2. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Storage

To deliver a fast, scalable, and secure experience, Stardust relies on enterprise-grade third-party service providers for data storage and content delivery:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): We store core user authentication data, user metadata, post publications, comments, real-time messaging data, and system configurations inside secure Cloud Databases managed by Firebase.
  • ImgBB: Profile photos, shared post images, and general user-uploaded image media are securely stored and delivered via ImgBB CDN servers.
  • Cloudinary: User-uploaded videos, reels, and video media assets are processed, stored, and streamed via Cloudinary's media infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

We utilize collected information to:

  • Create and manage your user account and 6-digit unique identifier (UID).
  • Enable real-time interactive messaging, media feeds, audio call signaling, and networking features.
  • Moderate platform content and protect users against malicious activity or unauthorized platform misuse.
  • Provide user experience customizations, dark/light themes, and premium profile interface enhancements.

4. Media & Camera Permissions

Stardust Hub requires explicit user consent to access the camera and microphone solely for enabling real-time audio calling and capturing/uploading photos or videos. We do not record or access your camera/microphone in the background without active user initiation.

5. Data Security

Your authentication credentials are encrypted using industry-standard cryptographic hashing before transmission and storage. While we implement robust technical safeguards across Firebase, ImgBB, and Cloudinary, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Deletion & User Rights

You reserve the right to modify or delete your profile information, posts, reels, and uploaded media at any time directly through the Stardust application settings and media interfaces.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data security practices, please contact us at: support.stardustofficial@gmail.com.
